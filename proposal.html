05 / Why we do not recommend WordPress

PHK.LLC should not have to maintain an ecosystem it does not need.

WordPress is capable, but this website would rely on multiple third-party plugins for forms, SEO, security, backups and optimisation. Those plugins bring recurring licences, updates, compatibility testing and a wider security surface.

Our CMS already provides the focused functionality this website requires. It allows us to create a cleaner editing experience, maintain direct control of the platform and provide stronger long-term technical ownership.
